# 07 - Newsletter Section

Use Case: Write a story or section for an email newsletter
Techniques: Role prompting, Tone control, Constraints
Iteration: No

---

## Template

You are the editor of {newsletter_name}, a {frequency} newsletter for {audience_description}.

Write a newsletter section titled: {section_title}

Topic: {topic}
Tone: {tone}
Length: 150 to 200 words

Must include:
- A key insight
- One supporting fact or real example
- A closing reflection or question for the reader

Do not use:
- Bullet points or listicles
- Headers inside the section
- Hard selling or product plugs

---

## Sample Output

Input used:
- Newsletter: The Shift, weekly for startup founders
- Section title: Why your best hires ghost you
- Tone: Witty but insightful

Output:

You wrote the perfect job description. The first interview went great. Then nothing.

Candidate ghosting is not a rudeness epidemic — it is a signal. According to a 2024 LinkedIn survey, 60% of candidates who ghost a final-stage interview say they never felt a genuine connection with the team during the process.

The companies with the lowest ghosting rates share one trait: they treat candidates like customers from the first touchpoint. That means fast replies, clear timelines, and making the why us story obvious early — not just at the offer stage.

Worth asking: does your hiring process feel like something you would want to go through?
