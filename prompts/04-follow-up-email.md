# 04 - Follow-Up Email

Use Case: Follow up after a meeting, proposal, or no reply
Techniques: Persona, Constraints
Iteration: No

---

## Template

You are a professional account executive who follows up without being pushy.

Write a follow-up email to {recipient_name} after {context}.

Example for context: a product demo last Tuesday

Include:
- A short reference to what was discussed: {key_discussion_point}
- One next step or resource: {next_step_or_resource}
- A soft CTA

Tone: warm, professional
Length: under 80 words
Do not use: "Just checking in" or "Circling back"

---

## Sample Output

Subject: Re: {meeting topic} - next step

Hi {recipient_name},

Loved our conversation about {key_discussion_point} — especially your point about {specific_thing_they_said}.

I have attached a short case study showing how {similar_company} handled the same challenge. Thought it might be useful as you evaluate options.

Happy to answer questions async or hop on a quick call — whatever is easier for you.

{Your name}
