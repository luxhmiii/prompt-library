# 05 - Product Description

Use Case: Write an e-commerce or SaaS product description
Techniques: Few-shot, Constraints
Iteration: No

---

## Template

Here are two examples of strong product descriptions:

Example 1 - SaaS:
Notion is the all-in-one workspace where you write, plan, collaborate, and get organized. It lets you take notes, add tasks, manage projects, and more.

Example 2 - E-commerce:
Built for the long haul. Our Merino wool tee regulates your temperature, resists odor, and feels softer after every wash — so you can pack less and do more.

Now write a product description for {product_name}.

Details:
- Category: {product_category}
- Feature 1: {feature_1}
- Feature 2: {feature_2}
- Feature 3: {feature_3}
- Target customer: {target_customer}
- Tone: {tone}
- Length: 50 to 80 words

---

## Sample Output

Input used:
- Product: BrewMate Smart Coffee Maker
- Category: Kitchen Appliances
- Tone: Friendly and confident

Output:

BrewMate learns how you like your coffee — then makes it before you even ask. Set your schedule once, and wake up to a fresh brew at the exact strength and temperature you prefer. With Wi-Fi sync, voice control, and a self-cleaning cycle, it is the last coffee maker you will ever need to think about.
