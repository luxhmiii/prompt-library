# 06 - YouTube Title and Description

Use Case: Generate SEO-optimized YouTube video metadata
Techniques: Role prompting, Structured output
Iteration: No

---

## Template

You are a YouTube SEO specialist who has helped channels grow from 0 to 100K subscribers.

Generate a title and description for a video about {video_topic}.

Target audience: {audience}
Main keyword: {primary_keyword}
Video length: {video_length}

Output exactly in this format:

TITLES:
1. One title with a number
2. One title without a number
3. One title as a question

DESCRIPTION:
- Hook line (1 sentence)
- What they will learn (3 bullet points)
- Call to action
- 5 hashtags

---

## Sample Output

Input used:
- Topic: Beginner investing guide
- Keyword: how to invest as a beginner
- Audience: 22 to 35 year olds new to personal finance

TITLES:
1. How to Invest as a Beginner (Step-by-Step 2025 Guide)
2. The Beginner's Guide to Investing — Everything You Need to Start
3. Is It Too Late to Start Investing? Here is the Truth

DESCRIPTION:

If you have ever searched how to invest as a beginner and felt more confused after — this video is for you.

What you will learn:
- The only 3 account types you actually need
- How to invest your first $100 even if you are scared to lose it
- The one habit that separates long-term investors from everyone else

Drop your biggest money question in the comments below.
