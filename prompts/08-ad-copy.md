# 08 - Ad Copy (Google / Meta)

Use Case: Write short-form paid ad copy for Google or Meta
Techniques: Few-shot, Constraints
Iteration: No

---

## Template

Here are two examples of high-converting ad copy:

Google Ad Example:
Headline: Stop Losing Leads to Slow Forms
Description: FormPilot cuts form abandonment by 40%. Trusted by 500+ teams. Free trial, no card needed.

Meta Ad Example:
Hook: Your follow-up emails are not the problem. Your timing is.
Body: Most reps follow up too late. ReachRight sends the right message at the right moment automatically. 3,000 teams close more with less effort.
CTA: Start Free

Now write ad copy for {product_or_service}.

Platform: {Google or Meta}
Goal: {awareness or clicks or conversions}
Unique value proposition: {uvp}
Audience: {target_audience}

If Google, output:
- 3 headlines, max 30 characters each
- 2 descriptions, max 90 characters each

If Meta, output:
- Hook: 1 sentence
- Body: 2 to 3 sentences
- CTA: 2 to 4 words
