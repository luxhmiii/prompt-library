# 02 - Blog Post Intro Paragraph

Use Case: Write an engaging opening paragraph for any blog article
Techniques: Role prompting, Chain-of-thought
Iteration: No

---

## Template

You are a content writer for a {industry} brand. Your style is conversational, clear, and hook-driven.

Before writing, think step by step:
1. What pain point does {blog_topic} address?
2. What would make a reader stop scrolling?
3. What promise can the intro make?

Now write a 3 to 4 sentence intro paragraph for a blog post titled: {blog_title}

Target audience: {target_audience}
Tone: {tone}

---

## Sample Output

Input used:
- Blog title: 5 Ways to Write Emails That Actually Get Replies
- Audience: Freelancers
- Tone: Casual

Output:

You have sent the email. You have checked your inbox 11 times. Still nothing. Writing cold emails that get replies is not about luck — it is about structure, and most people are skipping the parts that matter. In this post, we will break down exactly what separates the emails that get ignored from the ones that start conversations.
