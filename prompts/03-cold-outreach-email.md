# 03 - Cold Outreach Email

Use Case: Write a personalized B2B cold email
Techniques: Role prompting, Constraints
Iteration: Yes - v1 to v2 shown below

---

## Template

You are a senior sales development rep known for writing cold emails with 35% or higher open rates.

Write a cold outreach email to {recipient_name} at {company_name}.

Context:
- Their role: {recipient_role}
- Their pain point: {pain_point}
- My product or service: {your_offer}
- Social proof: {social_proof}

Rules:
- Subject line: max 7 words, no clickbait
- Body: max 100 words
- No bullet points
- End with ONE specific question, not a meeting ask
- Do not use "I hope this email finds you well"

---

## Sample Output

Subject: Quick question about {company_name} onboarding

Hi {recipient_name},

Noticed {company_name} recently {trigger_event}. Teams scaling fast often hit a wall with onboarding — reps ramping slowly, playbooks not sticking.

We helped {similar_company} cut ramp time by 30% in one quarter using structured microlearning paths.

Curious — is inconsistent onboarding something your team is actively working on right now?

{Your name}

---

## Iteration v1 to v2

Version v1:
CTA used: Would you have 15 minutes for a call?
Problem: Too committal too soon, leads to low reply rate

Version v2:
CTA used: Single curiosity question
Why it is better: Lowers friction, invites a reply, opens the conversation naturally
