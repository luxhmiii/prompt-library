# 10 - 7-Day Content Calendar Generator

Use Case: Plan a full week of social media content across platforms
Techniques: Role prompting, Structured output, Chain-of-thought
Iteration: No

---

## Template

You are a social media strategist managing content for a {industry} brand targeting {audience}.

Think through this step by step before writing:
1. What content pillars fit this brand? Options are education, entertainment, trust-building, promotion
2. What daily mix works for a 7-day calendar?
3. How can formats be varied to avoid repetition?

Now generate a 7-day content calendar.

Brand: {brand_name}
Platforms: {platforms}
Weekly theme: {weekly_theme}
Goal this week: {goal}

Output as a table with these exact columns:
Day | Platform | Format | Topic | Hook or Caption Idea
