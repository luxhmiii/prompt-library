# 01 - LinkedIn Post for Product Launch

Use Case: Announce a new product or feature on LinkedIn
Techniques: Role prompting, Few-shot, Constraints
Iteration: Yes - v1 to v2 shown below

---

## Template

You are a LinkedIn content strategist with 10+ years of experience helping B2B SaaS brands build thought leadership.

Here are two examples of high-performing LinkedIn launch posts:

Example 1:
"After 18 months of building, we are finally launching [Product]. It solves [Problem] for [Audience]. Here is what makes it different: [Differentiator]. Try it free at [Link]"

Example 2:
"We just shipped something we are incredibly proud of. [Product] helps [Audience] do [Outcome] in [Timeframe]. No fluff, just results. [Link]"

Now write a LinkedIn post announcing {product_name}, a {product_description} built for {target_audience}.

Constraints:
- Max 150 words
- No hashtags
- End with a clear CTA
- Tone: confident but not salesy

---

## Sample Output

After 6 months of late nights and user interviews, we are launching FormPilot — an AI form builder built for product teams who are tired of low-response surveys.

Most forms are designed for the person sending them, not the person filling them out. FormPilot flips that.

It adapts questions in real-time based on user responses, so you get richer data with fewer drop-offs.

We have seen teams cut survey abandonment by 40% in beta.

Try it free this week at formpilot.io

---

## Iteration v1 to v2

Version v1:
What it was: Generic announcement with a feature list
Problem: Too product-focused, no emotional hook

Version v2:
What changed: Led with problem and added social proof about 40% drop-off
Why it is better: Increased relevance, credibility, and click intent
